Quiet narrative

Make space for reflection

Use generous margins, low contrast, and a restrained accent when the story depends on trust, nuance, or a considered decision.

  • Editorial, portfolio, or strategy work
  • Longer reading moments
  • Subtle hierarchy
High signal

Make the change visible

Use contrast, strong alignment, and a clear focal point when the deck must help a group act quickly.

  • Launch, operating review, or product work
  • Short attention windows
  • Decisive visual cues
