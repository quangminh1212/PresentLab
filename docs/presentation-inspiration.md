# Presentation inspiration

The gallery's redesign follows presentation patterns that have proved useful in
real decks. It borrows the logic of the references, not their logos, screenshots,
copy, or proprietary artwork.

## Reference patterns

- **Apple product keynote** — the iPhone introduction frames one product as a
  combination of three familiar jobs, then gives each capability its own reveal.
  The gallery turns that into `cold-open`, `three-reveal`, `feature`, and
  `close` beats with a stage-like visual hierarchy. Source:
  [Apple Newsroom: Apple Reinvents the Phone with iPhone](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/01/09Apple-Reinvents-the-Phone-with-iPhone/).
- **Airbnb seed deck** — a short deck, one idea per slide, a problem-to-solution
  mirror, an auditable market calculation, a single business model, and concrete
  adoption channels. The gallery turns that into `problem`, `solution`, `market`,
  `model`, `go-to-market`, and `proof` beats. Source:
  [Airbnb seed deck teardown](https://www.pitchsidian.com/case-studies/airbnb-seed-deck).
- **Sequoia pitch structure** — purpose, problem, solution, why now, market,
  alternatives, business model, team, financials, and vision. The 45-slide
  sample story uses that spine while leaving room for research, operating model,
  risk, and handoff layouts. Source:
  [Sequoia: Writing a Business Plan](https://sequoiacap.com/article/writing-a-business-plan).

## Implementation rule

Every generated template keeps the same HTML contract and 45-page sample story,
but the story is now driven by eight attention modes: keynote, pitch, editorial,
strategy, research, product, manifesto, and culture. A family or style group
selects one mode; the mode changes the treatment of the narrative beats, while
the existing palette, background, and diversity profile continue to create
template-level variation.
